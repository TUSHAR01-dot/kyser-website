Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2026

At Kyser, your journey is personal — and so is your data. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

01Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, profile photo and login credentials.
  • Activity data: distance, pace, duration, route, elevation, workout type and achievements.
  • Location data: GPS data used to record routes and map your activities.
  • Device information: device type, operating system, app version, and identifiers.
  • Usage information: screens viewed, features used, and interactions with the community.

02How We Use Your Information

  • To record and display your activities, stats, streaks and achievements.
  • To power social features such as following, cheers, comments and community events.
  • To provide analytics such as training load and race predictions.
  • To improve the Service, fix issues and develop new features.
  • To communicate important updates, security alerts and (with consent) marketing.

03Location Data & Your Controls

Kyser only records location while you start an activity. You can disable location permissions in your device settings at any time, though route-based features will not work without them. You can also hide the start and end points of your routes from other members.

04Sharing & Disclosure

We never sell your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the app (hosting, analytics, notifications), under strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Other Kyser members, for information you choose to share publicly or with followers.
  • Authorities, where required by applicable law or to protect rights and safety.

05Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it by law.

06Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and other safeguards to protect your information. No system is completely secure, so please keep your account credentials private.

07Your Rights & Choices

  • Access, correct or export the personal data we hold about you.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Manage profile visibility and activity-sharing preferences in-app.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.

08Children’s Privacy

Kyser is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

09Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses limited cookies and analytics to understand how the Service is used and to improve performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

10International Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards to ensure your data remains protected.

11Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes and update the “last updated” date above.

12Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@kyser.app.
